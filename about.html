About Queen Usman

I write fiction about how people become who they are.

I'm interested in the things that become part of a person before they necessarily realize they are becoming part of them: what we inherit, what we learn, what we repeat, what we anticipate, what we desire, and what we eventually question.

My fiction explores these questions through people rather than theories — through families, relationships, power, desire, duty, mistakes, misunderstandings, and the choices people make when there is no clean answer.

My essays follow the questions that remain after the stories end.
