Or So She Thought

A 1990s royal psychological thriller about duty, desire, and the making of a self.

Elizabeth has spent her life learning what is expected of her. Her future is arranged. Her responsibilities are clear. The life ahead of her seems settled.

Then she begins to wonder whether the things she wants were ever entirely hers to begin with.

Enter the Novel

Why Or So She Thought

If you have spent your life learning what you are supposed to want, how do you know when a desire is actually yours?

Or So She Thought explores the quieter forms of influence that shape a person long before they recognize them as influence: family, tradition, affection, duty, repetition, expectation, and the simple habit of anticipating what others will think.

Elizabeth's problem isn't merely that other people have made decisions for her. It is that she has become very good at making them for herself.

About Queen Usman

Queen Usman is a novelist interested in how people become who they are—the things they inherit, learn, desire, obey, and eventually question.

Her fiction explores identity, desire, power, relationships, and the ordinary ways people are shaped by the worlds around them. Her essays follow the questions her stories leave behind.
