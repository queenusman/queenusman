Or So She Thought

1990s · Norwyn · Psychological Thriller

Elizabeth has spent her life learning how to live correctly. Her future is arranged. Her responsibilities are clear. The life ahead of her appears settled.

Then she begins to wonder whether the things she wants were ever entirely hers to begin with.

A psychological thriller about duty, desire, expectation, and the making of a self. Enter the Novel

Why This Story

If you have spent your life learning what you are supposed to want, how do you know when a desire is actually yours?

Or So She Thought began with an interest in a quieter kind of influence: the kind that does not always look like control. Family. Tradition. Affection. Duty. Repetition. Expectation.

Things can become part of a person so gradually that, by the time they begin making choices, some of those choices already feel obvious. Elizabeth's question is not simply whether she can leave. It is whether she can recognize what she is choosing.

About Queen Usman

Queen Usman is a novelist interested in how people become who they are—the things they inherit, learn, desire, obey, and eventually question.

She writes fiction about identity, desire, power, relationships, and the ordinary ways people are shaped by the worlds around them. Her essays follow the questions her stories leave behind.
