The Story

Elizabeth has spent her life learning how to live correctly. Her future is arranged. Her responsibilities are clear. The life ahead of her appears settled.

Then she begins to wonder whether the things she wants were ever entirely hers to begin with.

Elizabeth

A young woman raised to meet expectation with grace — and increasingly uncertain about where that grace ends and she begins.

Norwyn

A fictional kingdom in the 1990s, where duty is inherited like a title, and where the private cost of public composure quietly accumulates.

The Questions

  • What happens when expectation becomes automatic?
  • If something was taught to you, does that make it less yours?
  • Can you love someone without allowing them to decide who you should become?
  • What happens when rebellion becomes another form of obedience?
  • Can leaving actually make you free?
  • What does it mean to choose when the person doing the choosing has already been shaped by everyone around her?
